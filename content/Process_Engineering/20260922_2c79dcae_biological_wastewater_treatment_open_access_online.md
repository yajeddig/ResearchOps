## 🎯 Relevance
This resource provides free, high-quality educational content crucial for enhancing the knowledge and skills of process engineers and data scientists working in the water and wastewater treatment industry. It offers a comprehensive curriculum covering fundamental principles, unit operations, process modeling, and control, directly contributing to improved plant design, optimization, and operational efficiency (ROI) in industrial applications like WWTPs.

## 📖 Content
The website "Home - BWWT" (studybwwt.online) serves as a portal for the "Biological Wastewater Treatment Online Course Series," an initiative of the Global Sanitation Graduate School (GSGS) in partnership with IHE Delft and supported by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and the International Water Association (IWA).

The platform offers 18 open-access online courses designed for wastewater treatment professionals, academicians, students, and practitioners. These courses aim to provide deeper insight, advanced knowledge, and stronger competence in sanitary engineering challenges related to biological wastewater treatment. The series comprises 90 hours of video-recorded lectures, 213 presentations with 4,230 slides, and 9 open-access textbooks.

Each course is stand-alone and available for self-study, including video lectures, presentations, informal assessments, and additional study materials. The courses can be downloaded and studied in any order. Future plans include instructor-led online courses, on-campus courses, and tailor-made training options.

**Terminology definitions provided on the page:**
*   **Self-study online course:** Delivers pre-recorded lessons accessible anytime, anyplace, without a teacher, but may include self-assessments. (e.g., MOOCs).
*   **Instructor-led online course:** Offers individual guidance and support from an instructor, with interactive communication and grading. This option is planned for the future.
*   **On-campus course:** A face-to-face course with fixed content and syllabus, typically held on campus. This option is planned for the future.
*   **Tailor-made training course:** Customized face-to-face training prepared for clients, organized by GSGS partners at various locations. This option is planned for the future.
